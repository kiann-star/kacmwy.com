❓ Common Questions

Quick answers to frequently asked questions. Can't find what you're looking for? Just reach out! 💡

🎮 Do you develop games for both Android and iOS?

Yes! We develop casual games and utility apps for both Android (Google Play) and iOS (App Store) platforms. We ensure your app reaches the widest possible audience.

💰 How does ad integration work?

We integrate various ad formats including splash ads, rewarded videos, interstitials, and banners from top ad networks like AdMob, Facebook Audience Network, Unity Ads, and more. We optimize placement for maximum revenue.

🌍 Do you work with international clients?

Absolutely! We serve clients worldwide and are familiar with different regional requirements, privacy laws (GDPR, COPPA, CCPA), and app store guidelines across markets.

⏱️ What's your typical development timeline?

Timeline varies based on project complexity. Simple apps may take 4-8 weeks, while more complex projects can take 2-4 months. We'll provide a detailed timeline during our initial consultation.

🔒 How do you handle user data and privacy?

We take privacy seriously! All our apps comply with GDPR, COPPA, and CCPA. We implement proper data protection measures and are transparent about data collection in our privacy policy.
