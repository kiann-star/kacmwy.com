💰 Ad Formats We Support

Various ad formats integrated seamlessly to balance revenue and user experience. 🎯

🚀 Splash Ads

Full-screen advertisements displayed when users launch the app. High visibility format with minimal user friction.

🎁 Rewarded Video Ads

User-initiated video ads that offer rewards (coins, extra lives, premium features). Highest engagement with positive UX.

⏸️ Interstitial Ads

Full-screen ads shown at natural pause points (level completion, screen transitions). High fill rates and eCPM.

📊 Banner Ads

Standard rectangular ads (320x50 or 320x100) displayed at top or bottom of screen. Steady revenue with low impact.
